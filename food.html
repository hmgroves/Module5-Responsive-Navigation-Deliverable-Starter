Food and Dietary Supplements

At Snake Supplies, we only offer a diet of frozen thawed prey items. We do not sell live prey or repti-links.

We carry a variety of vitamin and mineral powder supplements to ensure your snake receives all the nutrients it needs. Please only use supplements if directed to do so by your veterinarian.

To add any supplements to your snakes meal, dust the prey item lightly with the powder after thawing.
